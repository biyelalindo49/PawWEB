Who We Are

Learning with compassion and purpose

Pawsitive Pet Academy is presented as an academy dedicated to learning about pets and animal care. Our website helps visitors explore courses, find useful information and learn about the academy.

We believe that learning about animal needs, behaviour, welfare and responsible ownership can encourage people to treat animals with greater care and understanding.
